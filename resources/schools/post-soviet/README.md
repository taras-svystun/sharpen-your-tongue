# School: post-soviet

Ukrainian and post-Soviet prison subculture: понятия, roles, масти, debts, and words you answer for.

- **Characters** speak Russian with феня, as in the sources. Examples: смотрящий за хатой, a cellmate, a newcomer, a guard.
- **Quotes** keep the language of their source, Russian or Ukrainian.
- **Debrief** in Ukrainian (agreement 9 in `docs/PLAN.md`).
- **Scope**: the sources set it. A rule from one colony or one decade is local until three independent sources from different places agree.
