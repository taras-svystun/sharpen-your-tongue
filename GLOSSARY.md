# Glossary

Sharpen Your Tongue: a daily trainer for quick wit under pressure, using closed worlds with strict unwritten rules as training grounds.

School jargon (смотрящий, хата, "Ebonics" slang) is not here: it lives in `resources/schools/<school>/terms.md` and `reference.md`.

- **school**: one training ground with its own reference, sources, language and characters. Now: `post-soviet`, `east-coast`.
- **scene**: one simulated conversation in a school, where a character tests or provokes Taras. Built around 1-3 items.
- **trap**: a type of manipulation or word trap, shared by all schools (for example a false choice). Lives in `resources/curriculum/traps.md`.
- **move**: a type of answer that defuses a trap (for example rejecting the premise). Lives in `resources/curriculum/moves.md`.
- **look-alike**: the trap, move or harmless situation most often mistaken for a given one, with the test that tells them apart. Every entry in `resources/curriculum/` names one.
- **term**: a word or concept of one school, with sources. A word lives in that school's `terms.md`, a concept in its `reference.md` (ID `ps:t:<word>`).
- **riddle**: a trap as one school poses it: a test question for a newcomer, with the answer its source accepts. Lives in that school's `reference.md` (ID `ps:r:<n>`).
- **item**: anything the database tracks and schedules: a trap, a move, a term or a riddle.
- **scope**: where a source's claims hold: place, years, institution, genre.
- **source catalogue**: a school's `sources.md`. Each source has an ID, genre, scope, reliability, link, access (can we read it), and what was checked. Not-yet-checked finds wait in its "Candidates" list.
- **reliability**: how far a source can be trusted. *High*: scholarly work, or journalism with named sources and documents. *Medium*: one first-hand witness, or a narrator in an entertainment format. *Low*: anonymous or made for shock. *Lyrics only*: the romanticised layer.
- **general / local**: a claim backed by at least three independent sources from different places is general; otherwise local.
- **independent**: sources with different witnesses. A book that retells another source's testimony is not independent of it; two hosts of one channel count as one source, but a guest's own story counts separately when cited by name.
- **reserve**: researched terms Taras has not studied yet. Its minimum is the reserve rule, agreement 12 in `docs/PLAN.md`.
