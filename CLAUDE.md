# Sharpen Your Tongue

A tutor and simulator for social intelligence, using Ukrainian and post-Soviet prison subculture as the training ground: read a situation, see its hidden rules, spot manipulation, answer provocations, and speak with confidence and precision.

- `GLOSSARY.md`: the project's words, including the jargon studied. Use them in code, docs and commits.
- `docs/PLAN.md`: the plan and what we agreed. Read it when starting a step.
- `docs/journal.md`: what each finished step did and why.
- `docs/adr/`: decisions that are hard to undo. Read before changing the data format or what is shared with Fluent.

## Learning lab

Taras is learning social intelligence: how status, words and responsibility work in a closed group with strict unwritten rules. Prison subculture is the case study, not the goal; every lesson should end with what transfers to ordinary life (work, negotiation, conflict).

Who does what: the AI writes the scenarios, the feedback and the code; Taras owns the ideas and the answers, and must be able to explain every piece. Explain at the level of the mechanism: why a rule exists, what it protects, what happens to the one who breaks it.

Content stays educational: understanding, recognition and defence. No glorifying crime, and no guidance for real criminal activity.

## Finishing a step

A step is finished only when `README.md` has a copy-paste command for every way to run the project.
